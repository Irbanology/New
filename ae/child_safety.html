Wibeit Child Safety and CSAE Policy

Effective Date: 20th August 2025

This page contains Wibeit’s Published Standards prohibiting Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE)

Introduction

Wibeit is committed to maintaining a safe, respectful, and secure environment for all users. We maintain a zero tolerance policy against any form of Child Sexual Abuse or Exploitation (CSAE). This policy outlines the measures Wibeit takes to prevent, detect, and respond to activities that violate child safety standards, in compliance with Google Play’s Child Safety Standards and international child protection laws

1. Age Restrictions and Eligibility

  • Children under 13 years of age are not permitted to use Wibeit.
  • Users aged 13 to 18 may use Wibeit only with parental or legal guardian consent.
  • Wibeit may take reasonable steps to verify age or consent when required by law.
  • Accounts found to be in violation of these requirements may be restricted, suspended, or terminated.

2. Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited on Wibeit:

  • Sharing, promoting, or distributing any content that depicts or sexualizes minors.
  • Soliciting or requesting sexual material involving minors.
  • Grooming or attempting to exploit minors in any form.
  • Distributing links, files, or material related to CSAE.
  • Impersonating minors or facilitating harmful activities involving minors.

Any user engaging in these actions will face immediate account termination and may be reported to law enforcement.

3. Enforcement and Reporting

Wibeit enforces strict actions against any CSAE-related activity. Accounts or content violating this policy may be removed without prior notice.

We fully cooperate with law enforcement agencies and child protection organizations where legally required.

If you encounter any suspicious, abusive, or harmful content, please report it immediately:

  • Email: privacy@wibeit.co
  • Support: support@wibeit.co

Please include any relevant screenshots, chat IDs, or timestamps to assist our moderation team in investigating effectively

You can also report content directly from within the app by selecting “Report” in the conversation options.

4. Moderation and Safety Measures

Wibeit uses advanced tools and community systems to maintain user safety, including:

  • In-app reporting and blocking features.
  • Manual and automated review systems to detect violations.
  • Immediate removal of harmful content where technically possible.
  • Secure data preservation for lawful investigations.
  • Regular audits to align with global child protection standards.

These measures ensure that Wibeit remains a trusted and secure messaging platform.

5. Commitment to Lawful Cooperation

Wibeit complies with all applicable local and international child safety regulations, including COPPA, GDPR-K, and other global child protection frameworks.

We cooperate with law enforcement, online safety organizations, and relevant authorities to identify and address any cases involving CSAE, while upholding user privacy and data protection principles

6. Parental Guidance

Parents and guardians are encouraged to:

  • Supervise and guide their children’s online activities.
  • Promote safe communication practices and responsible use of Wibeit.
  • Report any concerns via Wibeit’s official channels.

Wibeit promotes digital wellbeing and encourages families to maintain a healthy, safe online experience.

7. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in laws, technologies, or safety practices. Any updates will be posted on this page. Continued use of Wibeit after such updates constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8. Contact Information

For any questions, safety concerns, or reports related to this policy, please contact:

  • Wibeit Technologies
  • Website: www.wibeit.co
  • Privacy Team: privacy@wibeit.co
  • Support: support@wibeit.co
