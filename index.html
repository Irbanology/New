helpWhat is WibeIT? expand_more

Wibeit is a secure messaging app designed to protect your conversations with end-to-end encryption. It ensures that only you and the person you’re communicating with can read your messages, keeping your data private at all times

help Is Wibeit safe and secure? expand_more

Yes. Wibeit uses strong encryption and a privacy-first approach to prevent unauthorized access. Your messages, calls, and shared files are protected from third parties, including the platform itself.

No. Wibeit cannot read your messages due to end-to-end encryption, and it does not store your conversations on central servers, reducing the risk of data breaches.

help Why should I switch to Wibeit? expand_more

If you want full control over your conversations, better privacy, and protection from tracking or data misuse, Wibeit provides a more secure alternative to traditional messaging apps.
