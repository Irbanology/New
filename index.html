About us

At Wibeit, our mission is simple: to make communication private, secure, and effortless for everyone. We built a secure messaging platform and privacy first communication tool so your conversations belong to you and only you.

Who We Are

Wibeit is a team of innovators, engineers, and privacy advocates who share a single vision: to build an encrypted communication app that puts users first. We designed Wibeit to be fast, reliable, and safe, without sacrificing simplicity or usability.

Our Vision

We envision a world where online communication is free from surveillance, data mining, and unauthorized access. With our encrypted chat and private messaging, you can connect confidently, knowing your words, calls, and media are truly private.

What We Do

We provide a secure messaging app with end to end encryption to protect your chats, calls, and shared media. With Wibeit:

FAQs

Quick answers about our secure messaging app, encrypted chat, and privacy.

helpWhat is Wibeit? expand_more

Wibeit is a secure messaging application designed to provide private, safe, and confidential communication. With end to end encryption, only you and the person you’re communicating with can read your messages.

help How does Wibeit ensure my messages are secure? expand_more

Wibeit uses P2P encryption built on some of the best encryption algorithms in 2026. Messages are encrypted on your device before being sent and can only be decrypted by the recipient’s device. Even Wibeit cannot access or read your messages.

help Does Wibeit collect personal data? expand_more

Wibeit collects only the minimal information required to provide the Service, such as your phone number or email for account verification. Conversations and media are never stored on Wibeit servers and cannot be accessed by us.

help Is Wibeit free to use? expand_more

Yes. Wibeit is free to download and use. While premium features may be added in the future, core messaging and calling will remain free.
