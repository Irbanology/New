Child Safety & Protection Policy

Effective Date: 20 August 2025

Introduction

WibeIT is committed to providing a safe and secure environment for all users, especially children. This Child Safety & Protection Policy explains eligibility, protective measures, parental guidance, and the compliance standards we follow to help keep young users safe.

1. Age Restrictions

  • Children under 13 are not permitted to use WibeIT.
  • Teens aged 13 to 18 may use WibeIT only with parent or legal guardian consent.
  • We may take steps to verify age or parental consent where required by law.
  • Accounts violating these requirements may be suspended or terminated.

2. Protection Measures

  • WibeIT does not use targeted advertising or profiling for children.
  • All conversations are secured to protect privacy and safety.
  • In-app reporting tools let users flag harmful, abusive, or inappropriate content for prompt review by our team.

3. Parental Guidance

  • Parents and guardians are encouraged to supervise and guide their children’s online activity.
  • WibeIT promotes safe communication practices and supports digital wellbeing.

4. Compliance

WibeIT complies with international child protection laws and regulations, including:

  • COPPA
  • GDPR-K
  • Other applicable global child safety standards

5. Reporting & Contact

If you believe a child is using WibeIT without proper consent, or if you need to report abuse or safety concerns, contact us:

  • Safety & Abuse: privacy@wibeit.co
  • Support: support@wibeit.co
  • Website: www.wibeit.co

We may request information to verify identities and protect user privacy when processing safety reports.

6. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Policy as laws, technology, or our practices evolve. If significant changes are made, we will provide appropriate notice. Continued use of WibeIT after updates means you accept the revised Policy.
